# INSIDE / STRANGE SIGNALS ATLAS — Evidence Review 03
**Date:** 2026-10-08
**Scope:** Archival intelligence research, controversial claims, physics that once sounded implausible, noninvasive neural interfaces, historical psychology, and the user's goal of detecting pure (not silently articulated) inner speech with a phone/headset.

## What a source proves (four separate levels)
- **Document exists:** A declassified document authenticates that an agency wrote/received the claim. It does *not* authenticate the claim's truth.
- **Experiment occurred:** Someone tried an experiment. This does *not* establish causality or robust results.
- **Evidence replicated:** Independent teams confirm measurable effects under rigorous controls. This still may not establish the proposed mechanism.
- **Working sensor/channel:** A measurable physical information carrier survives to a real device and contains content sufficient to decode held-out words in new sessions. This is the necessary INSIDE gate.

## A. Intelligence records, not urban legends

### 1 — CIA / DIA / SRI: STARGATE / remote viewing
- 1970s-1995 work attempted perception of blocked/remote information using purported paranormal abilities. Original SRI investigators wrote positive reports, and Targ & Puthoff published a 1974 paper in *Nature*: https://www.nature.com/articles/251602a0
- The CIA acknowledges that it and DIA studied remote viewing: https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/ask-molly-did-cia-really-study-psychic-powers
- A CIA-commissioned 1995 AIR evaluation found statistically nonchance patterns in some laboratory tests but did not establish a paranormal cause, and reported no actionable intelligence; expert reviewers Jessica Utts and Ray Hyman disagreed about what lab results establish. https://theclassifiedrecord.com/documents/stargate/primary-documents
- **Relevance:** A hidden information channel is a testable hypothesis; blind targeting, independent judging and rigorous replication are essential.
- **Not demonstrated:** Telepathic word recognition by a phone, reliable psychic access to arbitrary facts.

### 2 — Army: Gateway Process (1983)
- *Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process*, 9 June 1983, is a real US Army report preserved by the US Government Publishing Office. It discusses Monroe Institute audio / Hemi-Sync, altered states, out-of-body conjecture, holographic metaphors and proposed mechanics.
- Government original: https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/GOVPUB-D101-PURL-gpo152943
- **Relevance:** An unusual historical attempt to synthesize acoustic stimulation with models of consciousness.
- **Not demonstrated:** Free thought reception, astral projection as a physical sensor method, usable frequency to read internal words. The archive preserves a report, not a scientific validation.

### 3 — CIA: MKULTRA
- Government letters and congressional testimony establish historical unethical human experimentation on behavior, including drugs and hypnosis, often on unwitting subjects.
- Original records: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp80m00165a000700140054-4 and https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp99-00498r000300020007-3
- **Relevance:** There really were hidden experiments. Keep ethics at the center.
- **Not demonstrated:** Telepathic reading or reliable remote mind control technology.

### 4 — USSR / Cold War psychotronics
- CIA archive contains historical survey *Parapsychology in the USSR*: telepathy, psychokinesis, "biofield", Kirlian effects, mental suggestion; the survey itself notes many claimed military efforts were unverified.
- Primary: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00792R000500210002-8.pdf
- CIA-archived SRI study discusses Soviet hypotheses about extremely-low-frequency (ELF) electromagnetic transmission for alleged telepathy: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp81b00401r002600010013-2
- **Relevance:** Search historically neglected hypotheses, but distinguish hypotheses from measurement.
- **Not demonstrated:** Human mind acts as an independent electromagnetic radio carrying words through air.

## B. Phenomena that sounded extraordinary but are physically real

### 5 — Microwave auditory effect / Frey effect (1962)
- Pulsed RF energy can produce clicks/buzzes perceived as sound. Mechanistic studies support rapid thermoelastic tissue expansion creating pressure waves that stimulate hearing through bone conduction. This is **externally induced sensation**, not transmission of another person's inner thought.
- Original: https://doi.org/10.1152/jappl.1962.17.4.689
- Review: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17495664/
- **Relevance:** The sound someone experiences doesn't always originate from a conventional loudspeaker. Search the *physical carrier*, not superficial interpretations.
- **Safety:** Do not attempt RF/microwave exposure experiments.

### 6 — Eye movement induces measurable eardrum oscillations (EMREOs)
- Brain-controlled eye saccades are associated with minute, direction-specific ear-canal pressure waves without any external sound. Requires specialized in-ear pressure measurement, not proven with commercial ANC headphones.
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5819440/
- **Relevance:** A physical signal can reveal neural motor commands in an unexpected organ.
- **Not demonstrated:** Internal sentence identity in eardrum movements.

### 7 — Kirlian photographs do depict a real effect
- High-voltage corona discharge is real; reported glowing photographic fringes predominantly track moisture / surface parameters, not validated evidence for a human thought aura.
- Experimental Science paper: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/968480/
- **Relevance:** A surprising image can be a real physical phenomenon while the proposed explanation is wrong.
- **Safety:** No DIY high-voltage photography on the body.

### 8 — Human brain-to-brain interface (2014)
- EEG motor imagery -> computer network -> TMS stimulation can send limited choices or trigger motor responses in a second brain in laboratory studies, not unrestricted private thoughts.
- https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0111332
- https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0105225
- **Relevance:** End-to-end brain information transfer can occur using explicit sensors and encoding protocols; no exotic sixth sense is needed.

## C. Genuine thought-decoding engineering

### 9 — DARPA N3
- The goal was wearable, non-surgical, bidirectional neural interfaces using optical, acoustic, or electromagnetic principles. DARPA clearly calls it a research goal; program now complete. This does not mean an ordinary phone accomplished it.
- https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/next-generation-nonsurgical-neurotechnology
- https://www.darpa.mil/news/2019/nonsurgical-brain-machine-interfaces

### 10 — UT Austin semantic decoder, 2023
- fMRI data and many hours of personalized training can decode the semantic gist of perceived or imagined stories as text, noninvasively, in consenting participants; requires massive stationary brain imaging and outputs aren't literal inner monologue transcriptions.
- https://news.utexas.edu/2023/05/01/brain-activity-decoder-can-reveal-stories-in-peoples-minds/

### 11 — MIT AlterEgo, 2018-19
- Designed for silent articulated intent, sensing residual neuromuscular activity around articulators; genuinely striking, but *not* a demonstration of decoding pure inner hearing that produces no muscular signal.
- https://www.media.mit.edu/publications/alterego-IUI/
- https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/bc5261bf-bd37-48b3-8a4f-461e653e419d

### 12 — Cortical inner speech neural decoding, 2025
- Implanted multi-unit recordings in four patients supported real-time decoding of imagined sentences and limited aspects of private free-form inner speech, with safeguarding against unintended reading.
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12360486/
- **Relevance:** The information really is neurally encoded; the unsolved device question is how to sense it cheaply and safely, and with privacy.

## D. The controversial experiments — positive and negative evidence

### 13 — Ganzfeld telepathy
- Some researchers' analyses find above-chance statistical effects; a 2024 registered-report meta-analysis argues a small overall effect across 1974-2020.
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11134153/
- Earlier analysis of 30 studies across 7 laboratories found no confirmed above-chance effect (1999): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10414223/
- **Status:** disputed, unresolved; no robust, reproducible mechanism or portable sensor for imagined sentence decoding is established.

### 14 — Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research (PEAR)
- Tested whether conscious intention could alter random physical devices; research group reported small statistical anomalies.
- Original lab research: https://www.pear-lab.com/publications and https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1550830707000584
- Independent replication is inconsistent. A large 2018 experiment with **12,571 participants** found Bayesian evidence favoring no effect: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5872141/
- **Status:** not evidence that thought can drive an ordinary phone sensor without known physical coupling.

### 15 — Modern high-transparency precognition replication
- 2023 Transparent Psi project (multi-lab) got **49.89%** correct vs 50% chance, not replicating the earlier **53.07%** claim: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9890107/
- 2025 PLOS ONE three confirmatory studies, **26,483 participants / 420,472 trials**: one study's below-chance finding temporarily replicated, then failed in the larger third test; no robust precognition mechanism was established. https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0335330
- **Relevance:** This is *how* INSIDE must evaluate extraordinary weak effects to avoid fooling ourselves.

## E. Decision for the INSIDE architecture

### Distinguish three projects
1. **Pure imagined words -> text** (original goal): needs word-identity-bearing physical measurements (neural, or an as-yet unproven peripheral channel).
2. **Silently attempted articulation -> text:** feasible using EMG / acoustic or ultrasound sensors in specific research setups, but different from pure inner hearing.
3. **Anomalous / nonconventional information access:** historically studied, scientifically disputed; cannot be assumed to solve either (1) or (2).

### Interesting hypotheses to keep
- **H-A:** Deep search for correlated brain-driven peripheral acoustic signatures (middle ear/cochlea) specific to imagined phonemes, *not* just attention, jaw, or eye movement. As of today **no primary validation found**.
- **H-B:** Optical/acoustic/magnetic sensing physics used by non-invasive neurotech; work backward from independently measured neural amplitudes and signal loss at sensor location; phone camera/mic cannot become sensitive to other modalities through code alone.
- **H-C:** Imagery content may be represented distributedly. Potentially fusing multiple *validated* weak signals could help, but fusing several channels without content information yields no inner words.
- **H-D:** A truly nonconventional channel would require preregistered randomized novel text (not visible to decoder), multiple independent labs, stringent blinding, and physical artifact controls. Don't substitute an impressive anecdote for a channel.

### Red lines
- A real declassified memo ≠ scientific verification of its author's worldview.
- A real unexpected physiological effect ≠ an encoded copy of a sentence.
- Non-significant pilot results, especially on N≈12 trials, must never be advertised as mind reading.
- Do not use DIY RF exposure, high-voltage experiments, or high-volume acoustic stimulation on/near head.
- Avoid overclaiming modern technology (fMRI is huge; TMS has stimulation risks; cortical arrays are implants).
- Retain informed consent, no covert audio or surveillance.

## F. Next round of research
- Survey acoustic-mechanical signals from the **middle ear** under *pure auditory imagery* and recorded physiological controls.
- Survey passive **ultra-sensitive magnetometry** (OPM / NV diamond sensors) with limits: magnetic sensitivity, standoff distance, shielding vs stock magnetometers.
- Survey neurovascular and biophoton ideas critically; require reproducible measurable code rather than metaphor.
- Locate neutral independent replication attempts for any claimed psychical/remote channel, and prioritize primary original records.
